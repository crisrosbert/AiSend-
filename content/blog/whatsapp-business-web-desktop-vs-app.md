## What Is WhatsApp Business Web?

WhatsApp Business Web is the web version of the WhatsApp Business app. It lets you access your business chats from a computer. You can use it through a web browser instead of handling every conversation on your phone.

With WhatsApp Business Web, you can:

- Send and receive customer messages

- View your business profile

- Use Quick Replies

- Manage your product catalog

- Organise chats with labels

- Share images, videos, and documents

Your actions are synced with your phone. So, when you send or receive a message on your computer, it also appears on your WhatsApp Business account.

This makes the web version useful for businesses that handle customer messages throughout the day.

## WhatsApp Business Web Login: Step-by-Step Guide


![WhatsApp Business Web Login: Step-by-Step Guide: illustration](https://app.performancemktg.net/covers/blog-whatsapp-business-web-desktop-vs-app-2.webp)
Logging in does not take long.

You only need to open WhatsApp Web and link your computer by scanning a QR code.

### Step 1: Open WhatsApp Business Web

Open web.whatsapp.com in your browser.

A QR code will appear on the screen.

### Step 2: Open WhatsApp Business on Your Phone

Open the WhatsApp Business app on your phone.

On Android, tap the three-dot menu.

On iPhone, open Settings.

Then select **Linked Devices**.

### Step 3: Link Your Account

Tap Link a Device.

Use your phone to scan the QR code shown on your computer.

Once the code is scanned, your chats and business information will start syncing.

### Step 4: Start Using WhatsApp Business Web

You can now manage your customer chats from your computer.

You can:

- Send and receive messages

- Use Quick Replies

- Organise chats with labels

- Access your product catalog

- Share images, videos, and documents

That is it. Your WhatsApp Business account is now available on your computer.

## How to Use WhatsApp Business Web on Windows

You can use WhatsApp Business on Windows through the desktop app.

Follow these steps:

- Download the WhatsApp desktop app from the Microsoft Store and open it.

- A QR code will appear on the screen.

- Open WhatsApp Business on your phone.

- Go to Linked Devices → Link a Device.

- Scan the QR code with your phone.

Once the code is scanned, your chats will appear on the Windows app.

## How to Use WhatsApp Business Web on Mac

The process is almost the same on a Mac.

- Download the WhatsApp desktop app from the App Store.

- Open the app and wait for the QR code.

- Open WhatsApp Business on your phone.

- Go to Linked Devices → Link a Device.

- Scan the QR code.

Your WhatsApp Business account will then open on your Mac.

## Common WhatsApp Business Web Login Issues and How to Fix Them


![Common WhatsApp Business Web Login Issues and How to Fix Them: illustration](https://app.performancemktg.net/covers/blog-whatsapp-business-web-desktop-vs-app-4.webp)
WhatsApp Business Web is simple to use, but problems can still happen.

The QR code may not scan. Chats may take time to load. Your device may also disconnect.

Most of these problems have simple fixes.

### QR Code Is Not Scanning

Try these steps:

- Clean your phone camera lens.

- Increase your computer screen brightness.

- Refresh the QR code.

- Scan the new code again.

- Check your internet connection.

### Device Is Not Syncing

Try the following:

- Refresh WhatsApp Business Web.

- Restart the WhatsApp Business app.

- Go to Linked Devices and connect the device again.

- Update WhatsApp Business to the latest version.

### Chats Are Not Loading

You can try:

- Refreshing the browser page

- Clearing your browser cache and cookies

- Turning off unnecessary browser extensions

- Opening WhatsApp Web in another browser

### WhatsApp Keeps Disconnecting

First, check your internet connection.

Then:

- Update your browser.

- Reconnect the device through Linked Devices.

- Restart your browser.

- Restart your computer if needed.

### Browser Compatibility Problems

Try using the latest version of browsers like Chrome, Edge, and others.If this issue persists, then clean your cache and cookies. You can also try opening WhatsApp Web in an incognito window. Most of the time, issues regarding login can be sorted by refreshing the page, upgrading application/browser, and internet connectivity.

## FAQs

### What is WhatsApp Business Web?

It is the computer version of WhatsApp Business. It lets you handle customer chats from a laptop or desktop instead of using your phone for every reply.

### How do I log in to WhatsApp Business Web?

Visit web.whatsapp.com on your computer. Open WhatsApp Business on your phone, select Linked Devices, tap Link a Device, and scan the QR code shown on the screen.

### Is WhatsApp Business Web free to use?

Yes, there is no separate charge for WhatsApp Business Web. You can use it with your existing WhatsApp Business account.

### Can multiple users use WhatsApp Business Web?

Several supported devices can be linked to one account. However, this is different from having a proper team inbox where several agents handle chats together.

**How is WhatsApp Business Web different from the app?
**The app works on your mobile device. WhatsApp Business Web enables you to use the same account on your desktop.

**Does WhatsApp Business Web support automation?
**Yes, you can use basic options such as Quick Replies and greetings messages. To automate conversations and chats, you can use WhatsApp Business API.

**Is bulk messaging possible with WhatsApp Business Web?
**You can use WhatsApp Business broadcast features, but they have limits. For larger campaigns, businesses can use the WhatsApp Business API.

### Is WhatsApp Business Web secure?

Your chats are protected with WhatsApp's security features, including end-to-end encryption. You can also check Linked Devices and remove a device that looks unfamiliar.

### Can I use WhatsApp Business Web without my phone?

A linked computer can keep working even when your phone is not being used at that moment. The main phone still needs to be used regularly to keep the account active.

### Which businesses should use WhatsApp Business Web?

Small businesses and solo business owners can use it to manage customer chats from a computer. It also helps when most of your work happens on a laptop or desktop.

### Can I make voice or video calls on WhatsApp Business Web?

Calling is available on supported computers and versions of WhatsApp. If the feature is available for your account, you can make calls from the desktop.

### Can I send documents through WhatsApp Business Web?

Yes. Documents, photos, videos, and other supported files can be shared directly from your computer.

### How do I log out of WhatsApp Business Web?

Open Linked Devices in WhatsApp Business on your phone. Select the computer you want to remove and choose Log Out.

**What will happen if I lose my mobile phone?**

Make sure that you secure your WhatsApp account as soon as possible. Besides, you should verify your linked devices and delete the devices which you don't recognize.

### Can I use WhatsApp Business Web with a regular WhatsApp account?

Yes. WhatsApp Web works with both regular WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business accounts. The business account gives you access to additional business features.

### How do I create a WhatsApp Business website?

WhatsApp doesn't provide a website for your business. You will have to create one by yourself. After that, you will be able to post its link in your WhatsApp Business account.

### What can I do if WhatsApp Business Web is not working?

Check your internet connection and refresh WhatsApp Web. If that does not help, check Linked Devices on your phone and link the computer again.
