## FAQs

### What Is a WhatsApp Broadcast?

It is a simple way of sending a single message to many recipients but without putting the recipients into a group. Each recipient gets a one-on-one message.

### What Is a WhatsApp Broadcast List?

A Broadcast List is a group of contacts you want to message together. You can create separate lists for different customer groups.

**How Many Contacts Can Be Added to a WhatsApp Broadcast?**

With the help of the WhatsApp Business app, you will have one broadcast list with maximum 256 members. However, for larger broadcasts, you should use the WhatsApp Business API.

How do I broadcast messages on WhatsApp?

Simply add your contacts into your Broadcast List, write your message, and hit send. If you are conducting mass campaigns, PerformanceMktg can assist you in broadcast management via the WhatsApp Business API.

### Do Customers Need to Save My Number to Receive a Broadcast?

Yes, customers generally need to save your number to receive broadcasts sent through the WhatsApp Business App. This is different for messages sent through the WhatsApp Business API.

### What Happens If I Reach My WhatsApp Broadcast Limit?

You cannot add more contacts once you reach the app's broadcast list limit. For a larger audience, you can move to the WhatsApp Business API.

### Can I Send Bulk WhatsApp Messages Through the WhatsApp Business API?

Yes. You are able to send bulk WhatsApp messages using the WhatsApp business API.

### Can My WhatsApp Business API Number Get Blocked?

Yes, because sending spam messages or violating WhatsApp’s rules can get your number blocked.

### Can PerformanceMktg Connect With Other Business Tools?

Yes. PerformanceMktg platform enables integration between WhatsApp with tools like CRM and eCommerce. It assists manage customer communication and automate workflows.

### How Much Does It Cost to Send Bulk WhatsApp Messages?

This is determined by the WhatsApp messaging service, [Meta's pricing](/blog/whatsapp-business-api-pricing-india), and the specific platform being used. In terms of API messaging, it adheres to Meta's pricing policy.
