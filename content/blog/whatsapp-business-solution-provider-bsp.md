> **Quick answer:** A WhatsApp Business Solution Provider (BSP) is a company approved by Meta to give businesses access to the WhatsApp Business API. It supplies the platform for campaigns, templates, a shared inbox and automation, so you can message customers at scale through the official channel instead of risking a [number ban](/blog/whatsapp-ban-reasons-and-fix).

# What is a WhatsApp Business Solution Provider?

Meta owns WhatsApp. For most businesses, the WhatsApp Business API is not something you plug into on your own. You connect through a partner, and that partner is called a Business Solution Provider, or BSP.

A BSP gives you the tools that sit on top of the API:

- A dashboard to send and schedule campaigns
- Help creating and submitting message templates
- A shared inbox so your whole team can answer from one number
- Chatbots and automation for common questions
- Analytics on sent, delivered, read, replied and clicked messages
- Billing for WhatsApp messages

The API itself is the pipe. The BSP is the platform you actually use every day. If you want the bigger picture of what the API can do, read our guide to the [official WhatsApp Business API](/blog/official-whatsapp-business-api).

# BSP vs the WhatsApp Business app

| | WhatsApp Business app | WhatsApp Business API via a BSP |
|---|---|---|
| Built for | Small shops, one or two users | Growing businesses and teams |
| Bulk campaigns | Very limited | Official campaigns with approved templates |
| Team access | A few linked devices | Many agents on one number |
| Automation | Basic away and greeting messages | Chatbots, flows and AI agents |
| Analytics | Basic | Per-campaign delivery and read data |

If you are weighing the two, our guide to [WhatsApp Business web, desktop and the app](/blog/whatsapp-business-web-desktop-vs-app) explains where the app stops being enough.

# Why use an official BSP instead of an unofficial tool

Browser extensions and unofficial "sender" tools work by automating the normal WhatsApp app or WhatsApp Web. WhatsApp's terms do not allow that kind of automation, so the risk lands on your own number.

A BSP uses the official API, so you work inside Meta's rules: approved templates, opt-in contacts and a quality rating that decides how many messages you can send. Our guide on [why WhatsApp bans numbers](/blog/whatsapp-ban-reasons-and-fix) covers the difference in detail.

# What a BSP does for you, step by step

1. **Gets you onto the API.** Sets up your WhatsApp Business account and number.
2. **Handles templates.** Helps you write, submit and manage templates. See [why templates get rejected](/blog/why-whatsapp-templates-get-rejected).
3. **Runs campaigns.** Bulk sends, scheduling and personalisation. Our [bulk WhatsApp messaging guide](/blog/bulk-whatsapp-messaging-guide) walks through a campaign.
4. **Manages conversations.** One shared inbox for your team.
5. **Automates replies.** Bots and flows for the questions you answer every day.
6. **Reports results.** So you know which messages work.
7. **Guides verification.** Supports your business profile and verification request. See [WhatsApp green tick verification](/blog/whatsapp-green-tick-verification).

# How to choose a BSP in India

Check these before you sign up:

- **Official status.** Confirm the company is an approved Meta partner and ask for proof.
- **Clear pricing.** Understand the platform fee and the per-message charge that comes from Meta. Our [WhatsApp Business API pricing guide for India](/blog/whatsapp-business-api-pricing-india) explains the message types.
- **A free plan or trial.** Test the product before you commit.
- **Ease of use.** Can your team run a campaign without a developer?
- **Inbox and automation.** Do you need chatbots and team routing?
- **Support.** Fast, local support saves hours when a template is rejected.
- **Integrations.** Your store, payment gateway and CRM.
- **Ownership.** You should keep your number and your customer data.

# What does a BSP cost?

Costs usually have two parts: the BSP's own plan, and the per-message charges from Meta. Rates depend on the message type, such as marketing, utility, authentication and service conversations. Because Meta changes its rate card from time to time, always check the current numbers before you budget. The [pricing guide](/blog/whatsapp-business-api-pricing-india) is kept up to date for India.

# FAQ

**What does BSP stand for in WhatsApp?**

BSP stands for Business Solution Provider. It is a company approved by Meta to provide access to the WhatsApp Business API and the tools around it.

**Do I need a BSP to use the WhatsApp Business API?**

In most cases, yes. You connect through a partner that provides the platform and handles onboarding.

**Is a BSP the same as a bulk sender tool?**

No. A bulk sender extension automates the normal app and breaks WhatsApp's terms. A BSP uses the official API approved by Meta.

**Can I keep my existing number with a BSP?**

Often yes, if the number is eligible. A number that is already active on the regular app usually has to be migrated first, and the rules can change, so ask your provider before you start.

**Is the WhatsApp Business API free?**

A BSP may offer a free plan, but Meta charges for many message types. Check the current rate card before you plan a campaign.
