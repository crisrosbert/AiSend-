**Quick summary**

- WhatsApp Web and Desktop support QR code and phone-number login.

- A WhatsApp account can be used across your phone and linked computers.

- Once your device gets connected, it won’t be necessary for your phone to be connected all the time to log into WhatsApp Web & Desktop.

- Sign-in problems can be very easily sorted out through internet connectivity check, WhatsApp updates, clearing browser cache, or connecting devices once more.

- If you wish to use WhatsApp for your business, [WhatsApp Business API](/blog/official-whatsapp-business-api) can be explored, which provides automated services and analytics.

WhatsApp doesn't only work on your smartphone; you can use your existing account on your computer via WhatsApp Web or the WhatsApp desktop app.

You can link your account via two methods: Either scan a QR code or log in using your WhatsApp phone number. After you have linked your computer, you can use WhatsApp even when you are offline from your phone.

This guide covers the different ways to log in, including Android, iPhone, WhatsApp Web, and WhatsApp Desktop. It also details what to do if the login fails, as well as how businesses can use WhatsApp if the usual WhatsApp Business application is not enough for their needs.

## How to Log In to WhatsApp Web With a Phone Number


![How to Log In to WhatsApp Web With a Phone Number: illustration](https://app.performancemktg.net/covers/blog-how-to-log-in-to-whatsapp-web-and-desktop-1.webp)
You may not have to scan the QR code every time you want to open the WhatsApp Web version. WhatsApp also offers a phone-number login option.

It is a quick procedure. You should follow the following steps.

### Step 1: Open WhatsApp Web

Open WhatsApp Web on your computer.

On the login screen, select Log in with phone number.

### Step 2: Enter Your WhatsApp Number

Enter the phone number linked to your WhatsApp account.

Click Next.

A login code will appear on your computer screen.

### Step 3: Enter the Code in WhatsApp

Open WhatsApp on your phone and enter the code shown on the computer.

Once the code is verified, your WhatsApp chats will open in the browser.

You can now use WhatsApp Web without scanning a QR code.

## How to Log In to WhatsApp Web With a QR Code on Android

The QR code method is still available and works well if your WhatsApp account is already active on your Android phone.

Follow these steps:

1.  Open WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business on your Android phone.

2.  Tap the three dots in the top-right corner.

3.  Select Linked Devices.

4.  Tap Link a Device. The QR scanner will open.

5.  Open WhatsApp Web on your computer.

6.  Use your mobile phone camera to scan the QR code.

7.  Once scanned successfully, you can log into WhatsApp on your computer.

Once scanned successfully, you will be logged in to WhatsApp via the computer.

## How to Log In to WhatsApp Web on iPhone

The process is slightly different on an iPhone because the Linked Devices option is found inside Settings.

Here is how to connect it:

1.  Open WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business on your iPhone.

2.  Open Settings.

3.  Tap Linked Devices.

4.  Press the Link a Device button to activate the QR code scanner.

5.  Access WhatsApp Web from your computer.

6.  Wait until the QR code pops up.

7.  Scan the QR code using your iPhone.

8.  Wait for the account to connect.

Your WhatsApp chats can be seen in the browser.

## Can You Use WhatsApp Web Without Keeping Your Phone Connected?

Yes, it is possible to use WhatsApp Web without connecting your phone with the help of the Linked Devices feature of WhatsApp. You still need your phone when you first link the computer. After that, the linked device can work independently.

To set it up:

1.  Open WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business on your phone.

2.  Go to Settings.

3.  Select Linked Devices.

4.  Tap Link a Device.

5.  Open WhatsApp Web on your computer.

6.  Wait for the QR code to appear.

7.  Scan the QR code with your phone.

8.  Wait for the devices to link.

After the setup, you can use WhatsApp on your computer even if your phone is switched off or is not connected to the internet.

This is useful when you spend a lot of time handling customer chats from a computer.

## How to Log In to WhatsApp Desktop on Windows and Mac


![How to Log In to WhatsApp Desktop on Windows and Mac: illustration](https://app.performancemktg.net/covers/blog-how-to-log-in-to-whatsapp-web-and-desktop-2.webp)
WhatsApp Desktop works as a separate application on your computer. You do not have to keep a tab open on the browser. It also supports features such as desktop notifications and lets you access your chats directly from the app.

Both WhatsApp Web and WhatsApp Desktop support QR code login and phone-number login. They also use WhatsApp's linked-device system.

### Step 1: Download WhatsApp Desktop

Visit the official download site for WhatsApp or look up WhatsApp from the Microsoft Store.

### Step 2: Download the Windows App

Choose "Download for Windows" if you are using a Windows system.

### Step 3: Open the Setup File

Once the download is complete, open WhatsAppSetup.exe from your Downloads folder.

### Step 4: Complete the Installation

Follow the instructions on your screen. After WhatsApp is installed, a QR code will appear in WhatsApp Desktop. In some regions, an additional option for logging in using your phone number will appear as well.

### Step 5: Link Your Phone and Computer

Now open WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business on your phone.

On an iPhone:

- Open Settings.

- Select Linked Devices.

- Tap Link a Device.

On Android:

- Open WhatsApp.

- Tap the three dots.

- Select Linked Devices.

- Tap Link a Device.

### Step 6: Scan the QR Code

Use your mobile to scan the QR code displayed in the WhatsApp Desktop app.

After you scan the QR code, your messages will begin syncing on your computer.

Now you can access WhatsApp Desktop without having to keep WhatsApp active in your web browser.

## WhatsApp Web Login Not Working? Try These Fixes


![WhatsApp Web Login Not Working? Try These Fixes: illustration](https://app.performancemktg.net/covers/blog-how-to-log-in-to-whatsapp-web-and-desktop-3.webp)
Sometimes WhatsApp Web or Desktop may fail to connect. Before trying anything complicated, check these common issues.

### 1. Check Your Internet Connection

Make sure your phone and computer have a working connection. A weak or unstable connection can stop the login process.

### 2. Clear Your Browser Cookies

In some cases, old cookies or browser cache may interfere with the WhatsApp web service.

Go to the browser settings and delete the cookies and cache associated with the WhatsApp web. After that, refresh the website and login again.

### 3. Update WhatsApp

Check whether your WhatsApp app has an available update.

Some login features may not work properly on an older version. Updating the app can also fix other connection problems.

### 4. Link the Device Again

If the computer is already listed under Linked Devices but is not working, remove it and connect it again.

On your phone, open:

**Settings > Linked Devices**

Select the device causing the problem and choose Log out.

Then open WhatsApp Web or Desktop and scan the QR code again.

### 5. Check Your VPN or Firewall

The connection might be disrupted by a virtual private network (VPN) or corporate firewall. If so, then turn it off and try to connect again. If none of these steps works, wait for a few minutes and try again. A temporary WhatsApp service issue can also affect login.

## FAQs

### 1. Can I log in to WhatsApp Web without my phone?

No, not while you are configuring the application for the first time. For setting up WhatsApp web, you will have to use your mobile. After configuring the app, you can use WhatsApp web without the phone.

### 2. Can I log in to WhatsApp Web without scanning a QR code?

Yes. WhatsApp also provides a phone-number login option in supported cases.

Open WhatsApp Web and select Log in with phone number. Enter your WhatsApp number and follow the verification steps shown on the screen.

You may still need your phone to complete the first login.

**3. Is it possible for me to sign into WhatsApp on two devices?**

Yes. It is possible to link multiple devices and use the same WhatsApp account on your mobile phone and the linked devices. You can do that via the Linked Devices option in WhatsApp.

### 4. Can I use the same WhatsApp number on multiple phones?

WhatsApp allows a main phone along with linked devices. If you need a business setup where several people handle customer conversations, a WhatsApp Business API platform can provide more suitable tools.

### 5. How do I log into WhatsApp from a browser?

Open WhatsApp Web on your computer.

Then:

1.  Open WhatsApp on your phone.

2.  Go to Settings on iPhone or tap the three dots on Android.

3.  Select Linked Devices.

4.  Tap Link a Device.

5.  Open WhatsApp Web on your computer.

6.  Scan the QR code shown on the screen.

Once the scan is complete, your WhatsApp chats will load in the browser.

### 6. What Is the difference between WhatsApp web and WhatsApp desktop?

WhatsApp Web runs inside a browser. WhatsApp Desktop is a separate application that you install on your computer.

Both let you access your WhatsApp account from a larger screen. Both also support linked-device features.

### 7. Is WhatsApp Business worth using?

If your small business has a moderate amount of customer conversations, WhatsApp Business is definitely worth trying out. However, if you have to handle too many chats, you'll probably require some additional functionality. This could include mass-messaging capabilities, automation, chatbots, and many more. Then, you should consider using the WhatsApp Business API.

### 8. How do I log out of WhatsApp Web?

To log out from the computer, open the WhatsApp Web menu and select Log out.

You can also log out remotely from your phone:

**Settings > Linked Devices > Select the device > Log out**

This is useful if you forgot to sign out from a computer that you no longer have access to.

### 9. Can I stay logged in to WhatsApp Web permanently?

You can keep a computer linked to your WhatsApp account instead of logging out after every session.

However, use this only on your own trusted computer. If you use a shared or public computer, log out after finishing your work.

### 10. Is it safe to Log in to WhatsApp Web?

### It is perfectly safe to log into WhatsApp web. However, avoid using WhatsApp Web from a public computer because your account will be easily accessible.

### 11. Can I use WhatsApp on my phone and WhatsApp Web at once?

### Yes, you can use WhatsApp on your phone and WhatsApp Web simultaneously.

**12. How do I resolve a WhatsApp Web Login problem?**

Here are some basic steps to be taken:

1.  Check your internet connection.

2.  Clear your browser cache and cookies.

3.  Update WhatsApp on your phone.

4.  Try another browser.

5.  Remove the linked computer from Linked Devices and connect it again.

6.  Temporarily turn off a VPN or firewall if one is active.

7.  Restart your phone and computer.

If the problem continues, try again after some time in case there is a temporary WhatsApp service issue.
