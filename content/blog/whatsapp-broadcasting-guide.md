## What is WhatsApp Broadcasting?


![What is WhatsApp Broadcasting?: illustration](https://app.performancemktg.net/covers/blog-whatsapp-broadcasting-guide-1.webp)
WhatsApp Broadcasting is a service which enables you to deliver one message to several clients simultaneously. The key point here is that the message will be delivered privately. Your clients will not know about other people receiving your message. Let us consider the following scenario. Imagine that you are running an online clothing shop and have 500 clients. You want to tell your customers about a weekend sale. Instead of messaging each person one by one, you can send one broadcast to the customers you choose. Each customer receives the message in their own WhatsApp chat.

Businesses commonly use WhatsApp Broadcasting for:

- Offers and discountsNew product launches

- Sale announcements

- Appointment reminders

- Event reminders

- Customer updates

- Lead follow-ups

- Product recommendations

- Re-engagement campaigns

In case of large campaigns, companies have the option to use the WhatsApp Business Platform for messaging their opted-in customers.

## How Does WhatsApp Broadcasting Work?

Using a WhatsApp broadcast, you can send the same message to all your contacts at once. Recipients receive them as a direct message.

There is no shared chat. There is no public list of recipients. If a customer replies, the reply comes directly to your business.

### Customer opt-in is important

Prior to sending any business messages to customers, ensure that your customers have opted in to receive WhatsApp communications from your business. You should not just collect the phone numbers and begin sending marketing messages.

Your opt-in procedure must make it clear to your customers that you will send messages from your business via WhatsApp.

Respect those who ask you to stop messaging them. This will make their experience better and also help you comply with WhatsApp Business Messaging Guidelines.

**WhatsApp Broadcast**

### WhatsApp Group

### WhatsApp Channel

### Differences Between WhatsApp Broadcasts and Channels

WhatsApp Broadcast lets you make direct communication. Each customer receives the message directly in their private chat window.

Channels are more like a public notice board. People subscribe to a channel and receive their updates in the Updates tab. They can react to posts or participate in surveys but not reply in any way as they can in chats.

It depends entirely on your needs.

- For announcements, brand updates, you should use Channels.

- For personalized messages, use WhatsApp Business API.

- Use Broadcasts for direct messages, replies, and customer conversations.

## How WhatsApp Broadcasts Work

WhatsApp Broadcast lets you reach several customers while keeping every conversation private.

Unlike a Group, there is no shared chat. Each customer receives the message separately.

Here are the main things to know before sending a broadcast.

### Opt-in Comes First

Send WhatsApp messages only to those customers who have consented to hearing from you. Without consent, your message could end up being marked as spam.

### Messages Go to Private Chats

A broadcast does not create a group conversation.

Each customer gets the message directly in their own WhatsApp chat. Other recipients cannot see it.

### Replies Stay Private

When a customer replies, the conversation comes directly to your business.

Other people on the broadcast list cannot see the reply.

### Saving Your Number Matters on the Business App

If you use the WhatsApp Business App, customers generally need to save your business number to receive your broadcast.

The WhatsApp Business API works differently and is designed for larger business communication.

**How to Send WhatsApp Broadcast with WhatsApp Business App?**

Small businesses can start with broadcasts using the WhatsApp Business App.

It is free. Next, there is no complexity in setting up. Moreover, it comes with a built-in broadcast feature.

One broadcast list can include up to 256 contacts. If you have a larger customer list, you can create more lists.

### How to Send a WhatsApp Broadcast on Android

1.  Open the WhatsApp Business App.

2.  Go to Chats and tap the three dots.

3.  Select New broadcast.

4.  Choose the contacts you want to message.

5.  Tap the green tick.

6.  Write your message and tap Send.

The message will reach each contact in a separate private chat.

### How to Send a WhatsApp Broadcast on iPhone

1.  Open the WhatsApp Business App.

2.  Go to Chats.

3.  Tap Broadcast Lists.

4.  Select New List.

5.  Choose your contacts.

6.  Tap Create.

7.  Write your message and tap Send.

### How to Check Your Broadcast

You can open Message Info to check the delivery and read status of your message. Double blue ticks usually mean the message has been read.

The Business App works well if you:

- Have a small customer base.

- Are testing [WhatsApp marketing](/blog/whatsapp-marketing-guide).

- Do not need advanced automation or analytics.

- Mostly message customers who have saved your number.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid When Broadcasting on WhatsApp

Sometimes a broadcast may not reach every customer.

Here are some common reasons:

### Incorrect Phone Number

The number may be wrong, inactive, or not linked to WhatsApp.

### Customer Has Blocked You

A customer who has blocked your business will not receive your messages.

### No Opt-in

Sending messages to people who have not agreed to receive them can cause delivery and account issues.

### Template Error

WhatsApp API campaigns use approved [message templates](/blog/why-whatsapp-templates-get-rejected). An issue with the template can affect delivery.

### Messaging Limit Reached

Your account may have reached its current messaging limit.

Check your account status and messaging capacity before running a large campaign.

## FAQs

### What is WhatsApp Broadcast?

WhatsApp Broadcast lets you send one message to many customers. Each person gets the message in a private chat. It does not create a group.

### How does WhatsApp Broadcast work?

Create a broadcast list and add your contacts. Write your message and send it. Each person gets it separately. Their replies come only to you.

### Can people see who else is on a WhatsApp Broadcast List?

No. People cannot see the other contacts on the list. They only see the message in their own chat.

### What is the Difference Between a WhatsApp Broadcast and WhatsApp Group?

A broadcast sends messages to people separately. A WhatsApp Group works differently. All members can see the group and its messages.

### What is the Difference Between WhatsApp Broadcast and WhatsApp Channels?

Broadcasts are for direct messages. Customers can reply to you privately. Channels work like an updates feed. Followers can react to posts, but cannot reply directly.

### What is the maximum WhatsApp Broadcast limit?

The WhatsApp Business App allows up to 256 contacts in one list. For larger campaigns, you can use the WhatsApp Business API. The limit depends on your Meta account and messaging tier.

### Can I broadcast to 1,000 contacts on WhatsApp?

Yes. But the WhatsApp Business App is limited to 256 contacts per list. For 1,000 contacts, use the WhatsApp Business API. Your contacts must also have opted in to receive your messages.

**What are the key disadvantages of WhatsApp Broadcast?**

The Business App has some contact limits per list. In addition, there might be issues sending your broadcasts if your customers haven't saved your number.
