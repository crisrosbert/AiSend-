## What is a WhatsApp business bulk message sender?

It is a tool that sends the same message, often personalised, to a list of customers. Businesses use it for:

- Offers and sale announcements
- Order and delivery updates
- Appointment and payment reminders
- Abandoned cart messages
- Event invitations

The risk depends on how the tool works. Some tools automate the regular WhatsApp app. Others use the official API. Only the second type is approved. [Read why numbers get banned](/blog/whatsapp-ban).

## Best practices for bulk WhatsApp messages

1. Send only to people who opted in.
2. Use clear, useful content. Offers, updates, reminders.
3. Segment your list. A message to everyone performs worse.
4. Add a way to opt out.
5. Send at sensible times and not too often.
6. Keep your templates short and specific.
7. Watch blocks and replies, and pause if they rise.
8. Warm up a new number before large sends.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is bulk messaging allowed on WhatsApp?**
Yes, through the official WhatsApp Business API with opt-in contacts and approved templates. Sending bulk messages from the regular app or an extension is not allowed.

**Can I send bulk WhatsApp messages from the WhatsApp Business app?**
The Business app has limited broadcast features and is meant for small-scale use. For large campaigns, use the official API.

**How many bulk messages can I send per day?**
Official API accounts have messaging limits that increase as quality stays good.

**Do I need opt-in?**
Yes. Opt-in protects your quality rating and follows the Business Messaging Policy.

**Can I send images and buttons in bulk?**
