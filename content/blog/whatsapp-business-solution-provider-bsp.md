**Quick answer:** A WhatsApp Business Solution Provider (BSP) is a company approved by Meta to give businesses access to the WhatsApp Business API. A BSP provides the platform for campaigns, templates, chat inbox and automation, so you can message customers at scale through the official channel and avoid the [ban risk of unofficial tools](/blog/whatsapp-ban).

## What is a WhatsApp Business Solution Provider?

Meta owns WhatsApp. Businesses cannot just plug into the WhatsApp Business API directly in most cases. They connect through a partner. That partner is the Business Solution Provider, commonly called a BSP.

A BSP gives you the tools you need on top of the API:

- A dashboard to send campaigns
- Template creation and approval support
- A shared inbox for your team
- Chatbots and automation
- Analytics
- Billing for WhatsApp conversations and messages

## Why use an official BSP instead of an unofficial tool

Unofficial senders and extensions automate the normal WhatsApp app. This is against WhatsApp's terms, so your number can be restricted. A BSP uses the official API, so you work within Meta's rules. See the full comparison: unofficial vs official WhatsApp API.

## What a BSP typically does for you

1. **Gets you onto the API.** Sets up your WhatsApp Business account and number.
2. **Handles templates.** Helps submit and manage message templates for approval.
3. **Runs campaigns.** Bulk sends, scheduling and personalisation.
4. **Manages conversations.** One shared inbox for your team.
5. **Automates replies.** Bots and flows for common questions.
6. **Reports results.** Sent, delivered, read, replied and clicked.
7. **Supports verification.** Guides you through the business profile and verified badge. See [WhatsApp green tick verification](/blog/whatsapp-green-tick-verification).

## How to choose a BSP in India

Check these before you sign up:

- **Official status.** Confirm they are an approved Meta partner. Ask for proof.
- **Pricing clarity.** Understand the platform fee and the per-message cost charged by Meta.
- **Free plan or trial.** Test before you commit.
- **Ease of use.** Can your team run a campaign without a developer?
- **Inbox and automation.** Do you need chatbots and team routing?
- **Support.** Local-language and quick support matters.
- **Integrations.** Shopify, Razorpay, CRM, your own API.
- **Data and number ownership.** You should keep your number and your customer data.

## What does a BSP cost?

Costs usually have two parts: the BSP platform plan and the per-message charges from Meta. Rates differ by message type, such as marketing, utility, authentication and service conversations.

## Frequently asked questions

**What does BSP stand for in WhatsApp?**
BSP stands for Business Solution Provider. It is a company approved by Meta to provide access to the WhatsApp Business API and related tools.

**Do I need a BSP to use the WhatsApp Business API?**
In most cases yes. You connect through a partner that provides the platform and onboarding.

**Is a BSP the same as a bulk sender tool?**
No. A bulk sender extension automates the normal app. A BSP uses the official API approved by Meta.

**Can I keep my existing number with a BSP?**
Often yes, if the number is eligible and not already registered on the regular app. You may need to migrate it.

**Is the WhatsApp Business API free?**
There can be a free plan on the BSP side, but Meta charges for many message types. Check the current rate card.
