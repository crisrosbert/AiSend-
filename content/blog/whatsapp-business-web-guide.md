This means that your business team may end up having to deal with a lot of chats via WhatsApp on a daily basis.

In this guide, we’ll look at how WhatsApp Business Web works, how to connect it, what you can do with it, and where its limits start to matter for growing teams.

### What exactly is WhatsApp Business Web?

WhatsApp Business Web is the browser version of your WhatsApp Business account. You can open it at web.whatsapp.com, link your account from your phone, and access your existing chats from your computer.

There is no separate account to manage. Your WhatsApp Business account stays on your phone, while the linked computer gives you another place to handle customer conversations.

This includes replying to messages, sending pictures and documents, using quick replies, viewing your products in your catalogue, and labelling your chats. For companies who frequently use WhatsApp for their communications, this can prove to be quite convenient for them.

## How to Log In to WhatsApp Business Web


![How to Log In to WhatsApp Business Web: illustration](https://app.performancemktg.net/covers/blog-whatsapp-business-web-guide-1.webp)
You can connect WhatsApp Business to your computer in a few steps. Keep your phone nearby because you’ll need it to scan the QR code.

### 1. Open WhatsApp Web

Open web.whatsapp.com in Chrome, Edge, Safari or another browser. You’ll see a QR code on the screen.

### 2. Open WhatsApp Business on your phone

Login to WhatsApp Business app on your mobile and navigate to Linked Devices.

- On Android, you may click on the three-dot menu first.

- On iPhone, you can navigate to Settings and then Linked Devices.

### 3. Scan the QR code

Click on the “Link a Device” button on your phone. Use your phone’s camera to scan the QR code shown on your computer.

Once the code is scanned, your WhatsApp Business chats will load on the computer.

### 4. Start using WhatsApp Business on your computer

You may start communicating with your customers via computer now. Send files and images, use quick replies, view your catalog, and organize your conversations with labels.

## Using WhatsApp Business on Windows

If you prefer to use a desktop application rather than your browser, you can opt for WhatsApp Business on Windows.

1.  **Install WhatsApp:** Download WhatsApp from the Microsoft Store and open the app. A QR code will appear.

2.  **Open Linked Devices:** On your phone, open WhatsApp Business and go to Linked Devices from the menu.

3.  **Link your computer:** Tap Link a Device and scan the QR code on your computer.

4.  **Start chatting:** Your WhatsApp Business conversations will appear once the device is linked.

## Using WhatsApp Business on Mac

The steps are almost the same on a Mac:

- Download WhatsApp from the App Store and open it.

- On your phone, go to Linked Devices > Link a Device.

- Scan the QR code shown on your Mac.

- Your chats will open once the connection is complete.

## How to Log Out

You can remove a computer from your WhatsApp Business account whenever you want.

**From your phone:
**Go to Linked Devices, select the computer, and tap Log Out.

**From the computer:
**Open WhatsApp, go to the menu, and select Log Out.

If you are using a shared computer, make a habit of logging out when you finish. It only takes a few seconds and prevents the next person from opening your customer chats.

## Can Multiple Devices Use the Same WhatsApp Business Account?

Yes, WhatsApp supports linked devices, so your WhatsApp Business account can be used on additional supported devices.

According to WhatsApp's current guidance, a standard WhatsApp Business setup can have up to four linked devices in addition to the primary phone. WhatsApp also requires the primary phone to be used periodically to keep the account active across linked devices.

But there is something worth clearing up here.

**Multiple devices do not mean multiple agents.**

Suppose four employees all need to answer customers using one WhatsApp number. Simply linking their devices does not give each person a separate customer queue.

Everyone may still see the same conversations. That can lead to situations where:

- Two employees reply to the same customer

- A customer message gets overlooked

- Nobody knows who is handling a lead

- Team members cannot easily track their own conversations

- There is no proper system for assigning chats

That setup may be manageable for a very small business. Once a support or sales team grows, it can become difficult to manage.

## Where WhatsApp Business Web Falls Short


![Where WhatsApp Business Web Falls Short: illustration](https://app.performancemktg.net/covers/blog-whatsapp-business-web-guide-4.webp)
WhatsApp Business Web works well for everyday customer chats, but it has limits once the workload or team gets bigger.

- **Limited linked devices
  > **The number of devices you can connect is limited, which can become an issue for larger teams.

- **No shared team inbox
  > **Chats are not automatically assigned to different agents, so two people may end up replying to the same customer.

- **No agent-level tracking
  > **You do not get a clear view of which team member handled a conversation or how each agent performed.

- **Basic automation
  > **If you need chatbots, automated customer journeys or advanced workflows, Web alone will not provide them.

- **Limited bulk messaging
  > **Regular WhatsApp Business is not designed for large-scale marketing campaigns and advanced broadcast management.

- **Limited reporting
  > **You will not receive any kind of reports related to campaigns, conversions, or team activities.

It might be fine when an individual or a group is handling the WhatsApp chats. However, as the number of chats increases, you might want to have a shared inbox, automation, integrations, and reports.

## FAQs

### 1. What is WhatsApp Business Web?

WhatsApp Business Web lets you use your business WhatsApp on a computer. You just link your phone by scanning a QR code in the WhatsApp Business app.

### 2. How do I log in to WhatsApp Business Web?

Open WhatsApp Web on your computer. Then open WhatsApp Business on your phone, go to **Linked Devices**, select **Link a Device** and scan the QR code on your computer.

### 3. Is WhatsApp Business Web free?

There is no separate subscription just for using WhatsApp Web. However, paid business platforms, integrations and other third-party services can have their own charges.

### 4. Can multiple users use WhatsApp Business Web simultaneously?

Multiple supported devices can be linked to a WhatsApp Business account. However, this does not give you a complete multi-agent customer support system with separate queues and agent assignments.

### 5. What is the difference between WhatsApp Business Web and the WhatsApp Business app?

The WhatsApp Business app is made for your phone, while WhatsApp Business Web gives you access from a computer.

### 6. Does WhatsApp Business Web support automation?

It supports some built-in business features, including quick replies and greeting messages. More advanced automation, such as chatbot journeys and CRM-triggered actions, requires additional WhatsApp Business solutions.

### 7. Can I send bulk messages through WhatsApp Business Web?

WhatsApp Web is not intended to be a full bulk marketing system. If you want to run larger WhatsApp campaigns, use an appropriate WhatsApp Business solution and follow WhatsApp's messaging and consent requirements.

### 8. Is WhatsApp Business Web secure?

WhatsApp provides end-to-end encryption for supported messages and calls. You should also protect your own account by securing your phone, checking linked devices and removing devices that you no longer use.

### 9. Can I use WhatsApp Business Web without my phone?

You need your phone to set up and link the account. After linking, supported devices can continue to work without the phone remaining connected to the internet all the time. The primary phone still needs to be used periodically to keep the linked setup active.

### 10. Which businesses should use WhatsApp Business Web?

It can be useful for almost any small business that communicates with customers through WhatsApp and has people working from computers.

It can be particularly convenient for ecommerce businesses, clinics, agencies, service providers, retailers and businesses that receive regular enquiries.
