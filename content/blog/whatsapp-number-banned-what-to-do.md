**Quick answer:** If your WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business number is banned (see [why WhatsApp bans numbers](/blog/whatsapp-ban)), stop sending messages, request a review inside the app, explain your legitimate business use, and wait for a response. If the ban stays, move to a new number on the official WhatsApp Business API with opt-in contacts so it does not happen again.

## What the ban message means

If you see a message such as "this account can no longer use WhatsApp Business" or that your number is banned, WhatsApp has decided that your account violated its terms or policy.

This is different from being blocked by one customer. A ban applies to your whole account.

For the full list of reasons, read our guide: [WhatsApp ban: reasons, fix and safe alternative](/blog/whatsapp-ban).

## Step 1: Stop sending from that number

Do not keep trying to send messages or reinstall the app repeatedly. Repeated attempts can make the situation worse.

## Step 2: Check why it might have happened

Common causes for business numbers:

- Bulk or unsolicited messages
- Many blocks and spam reports
- A browser extension or unofficial sender tool
- A modified WhatsApp app
- A new number that sent a lot of messages too quickly

Be honest with yourself about the cause. It decides what you must change.

## Step 3: Request a review

Inside the app, use the option to request a review of the ban. Keep your explanation short and factual:

- You are a legitimate business.
- What you use WhatsApp for (orders, support, appointments).
- You will follow the WhatsApp terms and Business Messaging Policy.

Do not send long emotional messages or repeat the request many times.

## Step 4: Wait for the response

Review outcomes vary. Some accounts are restored, some are not. There is no guaranteed timeline and no guarantee of reversal.

## Step 5: If the ban stays, rebuild the right way

Do not simply buy a new SIM and send the same bulk messages. You will likely get banned again.

Instead:

1. Get a new business number you control.
2. Switch to the official WhatsApp Business API through a Business Solution Provider. See [what a BSP is](/blog/whatsapp-business-solution-provider-bsp).
3. Message only opted-in contacts.
4. Use approved templates.
5. Ramp volume up gradually.
6. Apply for a verified business profile when eligible. Read [the verification guide](/blog/whatsapp-green-tick-verification).

If you were using a Chrome extension before, compare your options in unofficial vs official WhatsApp API.

## How to avoid a second ban

| Do | Do not |
|---|---|
| Collect opt-in from customers | Send to bought or scraped lists |
| Use approved templates | Send identical text to hundreds of chats |
| Segment and personalise | Use extensions or modified apps |
| Offer an easy opt-out | Ignore stop requests |
| Track blocks and replies | Ramp from zero to thousands overnight |

## Frequently asked questions

**How long does a WhatsApp ban last?**
It depends on the type. Temporary restrictions can last hours or days. Permanent bans do not end on their own, and you must request a review.

**Can I recover a banned WhatsApp Business number?**
Sometimes. Request a review in the app and explain your legitimate use. Success is not guaranteed.

**Will a new SIM fix the problem?**
Not on its own. If you repeat the same behaviour, the new number can be banned too. Fix the cause first.

**Does the official WhatsApp Business API prevent bans?**
It greatly reduces the risk when you follow the policy, because it is the approved channel for business messaging. It does not remove the need for opt-in and good content.

**Can an extension cause my number to be banned?**
Yes. Unofficial tools automate the app, which the terms do not allow, and your number takes the risk.
