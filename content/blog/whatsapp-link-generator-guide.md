## What Is a WhatsApp Link?


![What Is a WhatsApp Link?: illustration](https://app.performancemktg.net/covers/blog-whatsapp-link-generator-guide-1.webp)
A WhatsApp link is a link that opens a chat with your WhatsApp number.

For example, you may see a Chat on WhatsApp button on a business website. When you click it, WhatsApp opens and takes you to the chat with that business.

The customer does not have to type your number manually. In many cases, there is no need to save the number first either.

You can also add a pre-filled message to the link. This message appears in the chat box when the customer opens it.

## 3 Steps to Create a WhatsApp Link for Your Business


![3 Steps to Create a WhatsApp Link for Your Business: illustration](https://app.performancemktg.net/covers/blog-whatsapp-link-generator-guide-2.webp)
Creating a WhatsApp link is very easy. You just need to follow the following steps.

### Step 1: Enter Your WhatsApp Number

Select the country code and input the number on WhatsApp. For an Indian number, select “+91” and enter the phone number. Double-check the number before creating the link to ensure that the customers connect with the right WhatsApp account.

**Step 2: Add a Message**

You can add a message that customers will see when they open the chat.

For example: **“Hi, I would like to learn more about your services.”**

Customers can edit the message prior to sending. If you do not wish to include a message, you may leave this field blank.

### Step 3: Generate the Link

Click Generate Link. Your WhatsApp link will be created.

Copy it and place it wherever you want customers to reach you.

Before sharing it everywhere, open the link once and check that it takes you to the correct WhatsApp number.

## How to Use a WhatsApp Link?


![How to Use a WhatsApp Link?: illustration](https://app.performancemktg.net/covers/blog-whatsapp-link-generator-guide-4.webp)
A WhatsApp link is not limited to your website. You can share it wherever your customers already spend time online.

Here are some simple ways to use it:

### Add the WhatsApp Link to Your Social Media Bio

Social media profiles are usually the first places that people visit before reaching out to a company. Include your WhatsApp link in your profile so that people can initiate a conversation without searching for your contact details.

If you have several links to share, you can also use a link-in-bio page and include WhatsApp along with your other business links.

### Share the Link on Instagram Stories

Instagram Stories can help you reach people who already follow your business. Add your WhatsApp link to a Story using Instagram's link feature.

You can also save the Story as a Highlight if you want visitors to find it later.

**Simple steps:**

1.  Create an Instagram Story.

2.  Add the Link sticker.

3.  Paste your WhatsApp link.

4.  Publish the Story or add it to a Highlight.

### Share Your WhatsApp Link on Facebook

You can add your WhatsApp link to Facebook posts, captions or other suitable areas of your business page.

For example, if you post about a new product, add a line such as **“Have a question? Chat with us on WhatsApp.”** Then add your link.

### Add the Link to Your X Posts

When you advertise your business through X, you have the opportunity to include your WhatsApp link. It is important that your message is brief, and let the recipients know what information you can help them with.

### Add Your WhatsApp Link to Pinterest

Pinterest works well for businesses that share products, ideas or visual content. You can add your WhatsApp link where Pinterest allows external links and use it to guide interested visitors to your business chat.

### Add the Link to YouTube Video Descriptions

If you create YouTube videos for your business, your video description is another useful place for a WhatsApp link.

You can add a simple line such as:

**Have questions? Chat with us on WhatsApp.**

Place the link below it so viewers can find it without going through your website first.

### Add It to Your Website

Your website is one of the most useful places for a WhatsApp link. Add it to your contact page, product pages, service pages or a visible Chat on WhatsApp button.

It helps visitors to reach out to you directly and get the details they need.
