# A link only helps if people see it

Once you have a WhatsApp link, the question is where to put it. The best places are the ones your customers already look at, at the moment they have a question. Here are seven to start with.

# 1. Your social media bio

Instagram and Facebook give you one link in your bio. If you sell through messages, that link can open a chat instead of a general contact page. People who like a post can ask about it straight away.

# 2. A chat button on your website

Add a button that says "Chat with us on WhatsApp" and link it to your WhatsApp link. Put it where visitors hesitate: on product pages, on pricing and next to your contact details. It replaces a form that people may never finish.

# 3. Your Google Business Profile

People who search for a business near them often want one thing: a quick answer. Add your WhatsApp link wherever your profile lets you add a link, so they can message you from the search result.

# 4. Your email signature

Every email you send is a place to be reached. A line such as "Message me on WhatsApp" under your name gives customers a faster option than waiting for an email reply.

# 5. Ads and posts

Meta lets you run ads that open a WhatsApp chat when someone taps them. You can also add your link to the caption or comments of an ordinary post. In both cases, write a first message that names the offer, so you know what the person is asking about.

# 6. Printed material

Turn the link into a [QR code](/tools/whatsapp-link-generator) and put it on menus, packaging, flyers, shop windows and business cards. Add a short line saying what scanning does.

# 7. Invoices, receipts and order messages

After a purchase, customers often have questions about delivery, returns or payment. A link in the confirmation message or on the invoice sends those questions to a place where you can answer them.

# Find out which place works

If every place uses the same link, you will not know which one brings people. The fix is simple: change the opening message for each place.

| Place | Opening message |
|-------|-----------------|
| Instagram bio | Hi, I found you on Instagram |
| Website button | Hi, I am on your website and have a question |
| Google profile | Hi, I found you on Google |
| QR code on packaging | Hi, I scanned the code on my order |

The message arrives as the first line of the chat, so you can see where each customer came from. You can write and test all of these in the [WhatsApp link generator](/tools/whatsapp-link-generator).

# What happens after the tap

When someone messages your business, a 24-hour window opens in which you can reply freely if you use the WhatsApp Business API. Replying inside that window keeps the conversation simple, so aim to answer quickly.
