# Best Practices for WhatsApp Marketing


![Best Practices for WhatsApp Marketing: illustration](https://app.performancemktg.net/covers/blog-whatsapp-marketing-guide-5.webp)
WhatsApp marketing is not about sending many messages. It is about sending the right messages to the right people.

## Build Permission-Based Lists

Messages should only be sent to those that have subscribed. This will ensure that your campaigns stay relevant and safe sender reputation.

## Audience Segment

Not all customers want to receive the same messages. Organize contacts according to their interest, buying pattern, geographical location or customer status. And then send out meaningful messages to them.

## Personalise Your Messages

A little personalisation can go a long way.

Use names, previous purchases, preferences, or other relevant details when appropriate.

## Pick the Right Time

Timing matters.

Test different days and times. Check your campaign data. Find when your audience is most likely to respond.

## Keep Messages Short

WhatsApp is not the place for a five-page essay.

Get to the point.

Share the value. Then give customers a clear next step.

## Use Clear CTAs

Tell people what to do next.

**Shop Now. Book a Demo. Reply to Order. Get a Quote.**

Simple works.

Yes, it is possible to configure basic [WhatsApp automation](/blog/whatsapp-automation-guide) for free for your business using the tools provided by the WhatsApp Business application.

**What is WhatsApp's 24 Hour Rule?**

WhatsApp's 24-Hour Rule involves the provision by which companies can communicate unlimited text messages in the period of up to 24 hours after customers' communication with them.
